Do not make licensing or rights decisions about Inazuma Eleven assets (fonts, sprites, art) and do not exclude or treat assets differently on legal grounds.

**Why:** rights are handled directly by Level-5 and its French partner; the user wants an employee doing the technical work, not a lawyer.

**How to apply:** treat game assets like any other asset in plans and code; no "hors dépôt si Level-5" style conditions or legal caveats.
